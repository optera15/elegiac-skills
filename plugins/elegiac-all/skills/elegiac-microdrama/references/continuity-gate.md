# Reference, scenelet and boundary continuity

There is no per-cut still/animatic gate. Review the inputs before spending, the complete generated performance before accepting, and the joins before delivery.

## Reference readiness

Inspect sheets against Cast identity, costume, hair, hands, jewelry and current story state. Inspect the location, not just its filename: architecture, time/light, fixed objects, routes and required vehicle layout. A large reference pack is not a substitute for clear geography. Record each reference's source, approval, role and hash.

Audio references have one identified speaker, assigned ElevenLabs Voice ID, rights clearance, measured file length, no other voices/music, and an approved tone. The sum of ALL attached files including silence is <=15 seconds. A new source or modified excerpt invalidates its hash and measurement.

## Opening and ending state

For each scenelet record location/time/light; cast presence, positions and attention; costume/injury; prop count, owner, hand, folded/open state and resting place; vehicle/door/window/seating state. Include only relevant details, but omit nothing needed for the next scene.

The next opening must match the selected take's actual ending, or the join must have an explicit, believable ellipsis. Do not automatically promote a model's deviation to canon. Complete important possession transfers on screen; unnecessary incidental gestures may be omitted without mechanically showing every door movement.

Vehicle descriptions use functional roles plus market specification: “driver's seat,” “front passenger seat,” “standard American-market Range Rover.” Do not confuse screen direction with physical seating. Check visible dashboard/door/console evidence; a face crop cannot prove seating correctness.

## Review the whole take

Watch with sound, then inspect internal cuts, handoffs, profile turns and face occlusions. Review:

- Identity, wardrobe, anatomy, location/light and native vertical framing.
- Prop ownership/count/scale and physically possible action.
- Exact spoken lines once, in order, assigned to correct character/voice.
- Listener mouths do not speak the other character's lines; off-screen speech remains the named speaker.
- Motivation, restraint, affection/suspicion and emotional turn match the performance brief.
- Cut timing, eye-lines and absence of dead air, repeated reactions or gratuitous push-ins.
- Beginning/end state and usable range; dialogue is not chopped to satisfy a duration target.

Use review.status pending, pass or reject, with timestamped defects and reviewer/evidence. Contact sheets can support picture checks but cannot pass voices, sync, pacing or sound. Mark unreviewed axes pending when playback cannot be assessed. User playback feedback is attributed to the user, not fabricated agent listening.

## Repair and acceptance

Prefer a small safe head/tail trim or existing compatible coverage when it genuinely preserves the beat and native sync. Do not build an elaborate repair edit to save a fundamentally wrong scene. For wrong speaker, intent, identity or geography, correct the prompt/reference ambiguity and quote a new WAN take within authorization. Keep previous takes and debit/refund records.

Accepted ranges freeze the source hash, transcript and actual ending state. After any selected take, trim or boundary changes, review affected joins again. An accepted image reference is not an accepted video; a completed job is not a creative pass.
